# Singapore Travel Guide
Source: Wikivoyage Singapore Travel Guide
URL: https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Singapore

## Overview
Singapore is a city-state island nation in Southeast Asia, located at the southern tip of the Malay Peninsula. It is one of the world's most prosperous countries, with a highly developed economy and a multicultural population. The city is clean, safe, and efficient — an excellent destination for first-time Asia visitors.

## Districts and Neighbourhoods

### Marina Bay & Colonial Core
The heart of modern Singapore. Home to the iconic Marina Bay Sands hotel and casino, the Merlion statue, Gardens by the Bay, and many colonial-era buildings. The Civic District contains the National Museum, Fort Canning Park, and the Esplanade arts centre.

### Chinatown
A vibrant neighbourhood with heritage shophouses, temples, and excellent street food. Key sights include the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Sri Mariamman Temple, and the Chinatown Heritage Centre. The Maxwell Food Centre offers some of Singapore's best hawker food.

### Little India
Centred on Serangoon Road, this neighbourhood is full of colour, temples, and the smells of spices. Must-sees include Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, Mustafa Centre (24-hour shopping), and the Tekka Centre wet market and food court.

### Kampong Glam (Arab Street)
A Malay-Arab quarter featuring the golden-domed Sultan Mosque, Malay Heritage Centre, and trendy Haji Lane with boutique shops and cafes. Muscat Street and Arab Street sell textiles, perfumes, and traditional goods.

### Orchard Road
Singapore's premier shopping belt, lined with dozens of malls including ION Orchard, Ngee Ann City, and Mandarin Gallery. Great for luxury brands and international chains.

### Sentosa Island
A resort island off the southern tip of Singapore. Home to Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium, Adventure Cove Waterpark, Palawan Beach, Siloso Beach, and the iFly indoor skydiving centre.

### East Coast
A relaxed coastal neighbourhood with East Coast Park — popular for cycling, barbecuing, and seafood restaurants. Katong/Joo Chiat area has beautiful Peranakan shophouses and cuisine.

### Bukit Timah / Dempsey Hill
Bukit Timah Nature Reserve is the place for nature walks and seeing monkeys. Nearby Dempsey Hill is a cluster of former barracks now housing boutique restaurants and shops.

### Jurong / Western Singapore
Home to the Science Centre Singapore, Jurong Bird Park (now Bird Paradise in Mandai), and Jurong Lake Gardens.

### Northern Singapore / Mandai
The Mandai Wildlife Reserve area hosts the world-famous Singapore Zoo, Night Safari, River Wonders (formerly River Safari), and Bird Paradise.

## Major Attractions

### Gardens by the Bay
One of Singapore's most iconic attractions, featuring the spectacular Supertree Grove, Cloud Forest dome (cool misty rainforest), and Flower Dome (world's largest glass greenhouse). The OCBC Skyway walkway provides panoramic views. The Supertree light show occurs nightly at 7:45pm and 8:45pm. Admission to outdoor gardens is free; domes require paid tickets.

### Marina Bay Sands
Iconic integrated resort with a rooftop infinity pool (hotel guests only), SkyPark Observation Deck (public access with ticket), casino, luxury shopping mall, and ArtScience Museum. The nightly light and water show "Spectra" at the waterfront promenade is free.

### Singapore Zoo
One of the world's best open-concept zoos, where animals roam in naturalistic habitats separated from visitors by moats. Home to over 2,800 animals. Famous for its free-ranging orangutans.

### Night Safari
The world's first nocturnal wildlife park, open in the evening. Tram rides and walking trails allow visitors to see nocturnal animals in near-natural settings. Highly recommended experience.

### Universal Studios Singapore
Southeast Asia's first Universal Studios theme park, located on Sentosa Island. Features movie-themed rides and attractions including Jurassic World, Transformers, and Minion Land.

### Merlion Park
Free iconic attraction at the mouth of the Singapore River. The half-fish, half-lion statue is the symbol of Singapore. Great views of Marina Bay Sands and the city skyline.

### Fort Canning Park
Historic park on a hill in the heart of the city. Site of ancient Malay royalty, British colonial headquarters, and WWII operations. Now home to sculptures, gardens, a spice garden, and performance venues.

### National Museum of Singapore
The oldest museum in Singapore, housed in a stunning colonial building. Excellent exhibitions on Singapore's history, culture, and identity. Free admission on Friday evenings.

### ArtScience Museum
Distinctive lotus-shaped museum at Marina Bay Sands. Hosts major international touring exhibitions alongside permanent digital art installations. Suitable for all ages.

### Chinatown Heritage Centre
Explores the history of Chinese immigrants in Singapore through authentic recreations of cramped shophouse living conditions from the early 20th century.

### Singapore Botanic Gardens
UNESCO World Heritage Site and the oldest botanic garden in Southeast Asia. Free admission (except the National Orchid Garden). Beautiful lawns, heritage trees, and the famous Orchid Garden.

### Haw Par Villa
Quirky free theme park featuring Chinese mythology and folklore depicted in vivid statues and dioramas. Unique and highly photogenic.

### S.E.A. Aquarium
One of the world's largest aquariums, home to over 100,000 marine animals. Located on Sentosa Island.

## Transportation

### MRT (Mass Rapid Transit)
Singapore's MRT network is fast, reliable, air-conditioned, and extensive. It connects all major tourist areas. Trains run from approximately 5:30am to midnight. Standard adult fares range from SGD 0.83 to SGD 2.16 using an EZ-Link card.

### EZ-Link Card
A stored-value contactless card used on MRT, buses, and some taxis. Can be purchased at MRT stations for SGD 12 (includes SGD 7 stored value). Highly recommended over buying single-trip tickets.

### Buses
Extensive bus network covers areas not directly served by MRT. The same EZ-Link card works on buses.

### Taxis and Ride-Hailing
Taxis are metered and plentiful. Grab (Southeast Asia's dominant ride-hailing app) is widely used and often cheaper than taxis. Fares typically range SGD 8–20 for most city journeys.

### Airport Transfer
Changi Airport is connected to the city by the MRT East-West Line (Tanah Merah transfer, ~30 minutes, ~SGD 2). Taxis cost approximately SGD 20–35 to the city centre.

## Food and Local Experiences

### Hawker Centres
The heart of Singapore's food culture. Air-conditioned food courts and open-air hawker centres offer dozens of stalls selling local dishes at very affordable prices (SGD 3–6 per dish).

**Top Hawker Centres:**
- **Maxwell Food Centre** (Chinatown) — Famous Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice
- **Lau Pa Sat** (CBD) — Iconic Victorian cast-iron market, Satay Street at night
- **Old Airport Road Food Centre** (Kallang) — One of Singapore's oldest and most beloved hawker centres
- **Newton Food Centre** — Open late, known for seafood
- **Chinatown Complex Food Centre** — Over 200 stalls; try Hawker Chan (Michelin-starred chicken rice)

### Must-Try Local Dishes
- **Hainanese Chicken Rice** — Poached or roasted chicken served with fragrant rice cooked in chicken stock
- **Chilli Crab** — Stir-fried crab in tangy, savoury chilli sauce; iconic Singapore dish
- **Laksa** — Spicy coconut milk noodle soup
- **Char Kway Teow** — Stir-fried flat rice noodles with prawns, cockles, and Chinese sausage
- **Hokkien Mee** — Braised yellow noodles with prawns and pork
- **Satay** — Grilled meat skewers served with peanut sauce
- **Rojak** — Local fruit and vegetable salad with prawn paste dressing
- **Roti Prata** — Crispy Indian flatbread served with curry

### Dining Areas
- **Boat Quay & Clarke Quay** — Riverside dining and nightlife
- **Dempsey Hill** — Upscale al fresco restaurants in colonial bungalows
- **East Coast Road / Katong** — Peranakan food and cafes

## Cultural Tips

- Singapore is a multicultural society with Chinese (75%), Malay (15%), and Indian (9%) communities, plus expatriates
- English is the language of business and government; Singlish (a local creole) is spoken casually
- Dress modestly when visiting temples and mosques (shoulders and knees covered)
- Tipping is not customary in Singapore; most restaurants add a 10% service charge
- Chewing gum is banned from import and sale (though not from personal consumption)
- Littering carries heavy fines
- Jaywalking within 50m of a pedestrian crossing is a fineable offence
- Singapore is generally very safe; petty crime is rare

## Sample Itineraries

### 1-Day Highlights
- Morning: Merlion Park → Gardens by the Bay (Flower Dome + Cloud Forest)
- Afternoon: Chinatown (Buddha Tooth Relic Temple + Maxwell Food Centre lunch)
- Evening: Marina Bay Sands SkyPark → Spectra light show → Clarke Quay for dinner

### 2-Day Itinerary
**Day 1:** Colonial Core & Marina Bay
- Start at Merlion Park, walk to Esplanade
- Gardens by the Bay (Supertree Grove + domes)
- Lunch at Marina Bay area
- ArtScience Museum
- Evening: Supertree light show, dinner at Satay Street (Lau Pa Sat)

**Day 2:** Culture & Heritage
- Morning: Little India (Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, Tekka Centre breakfast)
- Afternoon: Kampong Glam (Sultan Mosque, Haji Lane shopping)
- Late afternoon: Chinatown (Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Chinatown Street Market)
- Evening: Clarke Quay riverfront dining

### 3-Day Itinerary
**Day 1:** Marina Bay & Colonial Core
- Merlion Park, Gardens by the Bay, National Museum
- Evening: Marina Bay Sands SkyPark, Spectra show

**Day 2:** Culture Neighbourhoods + Night Safari
- Morning: Little India, Kampong Glam
- Afternoon: Chinatown, Maxwell Food Centre lunch
- Evening: Night Safari (opens 7pm, highly recommended)

**Day 3:** Sentosa Island
- Universal Studios Singapore or S.E.A. Aquarium
- Beach time at Palawan or Siloso Beach
- Return to city: Orchard Road for shopping and dinner

### Family with Children Itinerary
- **Day 1:** Singapore Zoo (morning) + Gardens by the Bay (afternoon/evening light show)
- **Day 2:** Sentosa — Universal Studios or S.E.A. Aquarium + beach
- **Day 3:** Science Centre Singapore + Jurong Lake Gardens + Night Safari

## Indoor Attractions
For hot or rainy days, excellent indoor options include:
- Gardens by the Bay (Cloud Forest and Flower Dome are fully air-conditioned domes)
- ArtScience Museum
- Universal Studios Singapore (many indoor rides)
- S.E.A. Aquarium
- National Museum of Singapore
- Chinatown Heritage Centre
- Malls along Orchard Road (ION Orchard, Ngee Ann City)
- Mustafa Centre (24-hour shopping centre)
- iFly Singapore (indoor skydiving, Sentosa)
- Madame Tussauds Singapore (Sentosa)

## Outdoor Attractions
- Merlion Park (best at sunrise/sunset)
- Fort Canning Park
- Singapore Botanic Gardens (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
- East Coast Park (cycling, beach)
- Bukit Timah Nature Reserve (rainforest hike)
- Pulau Ubin island (old kampong village atmosphere, cycling)
- Southern Ridges (7km walk connecting HarbourFront to Buona Vista)
- MacRitchie Reservoir (boardwalk and treetop walk)

## Practical Information

**Currency:** Singapore Dollar (SGD)
**Climate:** Hot and humid year-round; average 25–31°C. Two monsoon seasons (Nov–Jan and May–Jul). Rain can occur any month.
**Best Time to Visit:** February–April (driest, Chinese New Year festivities)
**Electricity:** 230V, 50Hz; British-style 3-pin plugs (Type G)
**Water:** Tap water is safe to drink
**Wi-Fi:** Free Wi-Fi widely available; Wireless@SG network at public locations
**Emergency:** 999 (police), 995 (ambulance/fire)
**Tourist Hotline:** 1800-736-2000
