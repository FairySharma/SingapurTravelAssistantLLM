# Visit Singapore: Essential Travel Information
Source: Visit Singapore Official Website — Essential Travel Information
URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-guide-tips/

## Entry Requirements
- Most nationalities receive visa-free entry for 30–90 days
- Passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond travel dates
- Immigration and Checkpoints Authority (ICA) manages entry
- Arrival card (disembarkation form) must be completed; an electronic version is available at https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard/

## Climate and Weather
Singapore has a tropical rainforest climate with no distinct seasons. Key characteristics:
- **Temperature:** 25°C–31°C year-round
- **Humidity:** 70–90% relative humidity
- **Rainfall:** Average 2,340mm per year; rain can occur any day
- **Northeast Monsoon:** November to January — wetter with occasional prolonged rain
- **Southwest Monsoon:** May to July — afternoon thunderstorms common
- **Inter-monsoon periods (February–April and August–October):** Generally drier
- **February–April** is considered the best time to visit (lowest rainfall)
- Sunscreen, light cotton clothing, and an umbrella/poncho are recommended

## Language
- **Official languages:** English, Mandarin Chinese, Malay, Tamil
- **Language of tourism and business:** English; widely spoken and understood
- **Singlish** is a unique local creole blending English with Malay, Hokkien, and Tamil phrases
- Common Singlish: "lah" (emphasis), "can" (yes/okay), "shiok" (delicious/great), "kopitiam" (coffee shop)

## Currency
- **Currency:** Singapore Dollar (SGD, S$)
- **Banknotes:** S$2, S$5, S$10, S$50, S$100, S$1,000
- **Coins:** 5, 10, 20, 50 cents; S$1
- **Exchange:** Money changers at shopping centres and tourist areas typically offer better rates than hotel exchanges or airport counters
- **ATMs:** Widely available; most accept international Visa/Mastercard
- **Credit cards:** Widely accepted in hotels, restaurants, and large shops; hawker centres and small local shops may be cash-only
- **Tipping:** Not customary; restaurants add 10% service charge and 9% GST

## Connectivity and Communications
- **Country code:** +65
- **SIM cards:** Tourist SIM cards available at Changi Airport from major operators (Singtel, StarHub, M1); typically SGD 10–30 for 4G/5G data packages
- **Wi-Fi:** Free Wireless@SG Wi-Fi available at MRT stations, libraries, community centres, and many malls and cafes
- **Voltage:** 230V, 50Hz; Type G (British 3-pin) plugs

## Health and Safety
- **Tap water:** Safe to drink directly from the tap — Singapore has some of the highest quality tap water in Asia
- **Healthcare:** World-class hospitals and clinics; travel insurance strongly recommended
- **Vaccinations:** No mandatory vaccinations; Hepatitis A/B and typhoid vaccines recommended
- **Dengue fever:** Endemic in Singapore; use insect repellent when outdoors
- **Food safety:** Very high standards; street hawker food is generally safe
- **Crime:** Extremely low crime rate; Singapore is consistently rated one of the world's safest cities
- **Emergency numbers:** 999 (police), 995 (fire/ambulance), 1777 (non-emergency police)

## Getting Around Singapore

### Changi Airport
- Ranked world's best airport multiple times; terminals connected by Skytrain
- **Jewel Changi Airport:** Free indoor attraction connecting Terminals 1, 2, and 3 with Rain Vortex (world's tallest indoor waterfall), gardens, retail, and dining
- **Airport to city:** MRT ~30 minutes (~SGD 2); taxi ~30 minutes (~SGD 20–35); private car services available

### Public Transport
- **MRT + Bus:** Efficient, frequent, and fully air-conditioned; covers all tourist areas
- **Tourist passes:** Singapore Tourist Pass — unlimited MRT and bus rides: 1-day SGD 22, 2-day SGD 29, 3-day SGD 34
- **Apps:** SimplyGo, SG Buses, and Google Maps work well for navigation

### Getting Around by Foot
- Singapore's central areas are walkable, with covered walkways and underground connections in many areas
- The Civic District, Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong Glam are all easily walkable

## Shopping
- **Orchard Road:** Premier mall strip — ION Orchard, Ngee Ann City, 313@Somerset, Paragon
- **Marina Bay:** The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands — luxury brands
- **VivoCity:** Singapore's largest mall, next to HarbourFront MRT
- **Bugis Junction / Bugis+:** Youth fashion and street shopping
- **Mustafa Centre (Little India):** 24-hour shopping; good for electronics, textiles, and international groceries
- **Chinatown:** Souvenirs, traditional products, dried goods
- **GST Refund:** Tourists can claim 9% GST refund on purchases over SGD 100 at participating retailers via the Electronic Tourist Refund Scheme (eTRS) at the airport

## Useful Apps for Tourists
- **MyTransport.SG / SG Buses:** Bus routes and MRT maps
- **Grab:** Ride-hailing and food delivery
- **Google Maps:** Navigation and transit planning
- **Klook / GetYourGuide:** Book attraction tickets and tours in advance
- **Eatigo:** Restaurant reservations with time-based discounts
- **Hawker Centre Guide:** Find and review hawker stalls

## Tourism Services
- **Singapore Tourism Board (STB):** Official tourism body
- **Tourist hubs:** ION Orchard visitor centre, Changi Airport tourist desks
- **Tourist Hotline:** 1800-736-2000 (free within Singapore)
- **Lost and Found:** Contact Singapore Police Force (999) or specific venue
- **Hospital:** Singapore General Hospital (+65 6222 3322); Tan Tock Seng Hospital (+65 6256 6011)

## Cultural Etiquette
- Remove shoes before entering homes and some religious sites
- Use both hands or the right hand only when passing items
- Avoid pointing with your index finger; use an open hand gesture
- During Ramadan, avoid eating, drinking, or smoking in front of Muslims who are fasting
- Queuing is taken very seriously — always queue patiently
- Public displays of affection are generally accepted but keep it tasteful

## Singapore Pass / Attractions Passes
- **Singapore Explorer Pass:** Choose 3, 5, or 7 attractions from 35+ options
- **Sentosa Fun Pass:** Digital credits for Sentosa Island attractions
- Book attraction tickets online in advance to save time and sometimes money

## Sustainability
- Singapore is committed to environmental sustainability; the "Singapore Green Plan 2030" is a national initiative
- Recycle bins widely available; plastic bag charges implemented
- Many hotels and restaurants are moving to sustainable practices
