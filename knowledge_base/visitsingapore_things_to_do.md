# Visit Singapore: Things To Do
Source: Visit Singapore — Things To Do
URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/see-do-singapore/

## Nature & Parks

### Gardens by the Bay
- **Type:** Outdoor + indoor (domes)
- **Location:** Marina Bay; 18 Marina Gardens Drive
- **Highlights:** 18 Supertrees (some reaching 16 stories high), OCBC Skyway elevated walkway, Cloud Forest dome, Flower Dome, Children's Garden, Sun Pavilion
- **Cost:** Outdoor free; Cloud Forest SGD 14, Flower Dome SGD 14, combo SGD 28 (adult)
- **Best for:** Everyone; excellent for families and photographers
- **Tip:** Visit outdoor Supertrees in the day; stay for the evening light show at 7:45pm

### Singapore Botanic Gardens
- **Type:** Outdoor park
- **Location:** Tanglin / Buona Vista
- **Highlights:** 160-year-old garden; National Orchid Garden (over 1,000 orchid species), Swan Lake, Heritage Trees, Jacob Ballas Children's Garden (free for children)
- **Cost:** Free (National Orchid Garden: SGD 5 adults, free for children under 12)
- **Best for:** Nature lovers, picnics, morning exercise

### Bukit Timah Nature Reserve
- **Type:** Rainforest hike
- **Highlights:** Singapore's highest hill (163.63m); primary rainforest; monkeys, monitor lizards
- **Cost:** Free
- **Best for:** Nature enthusiasts, hikers; recommended for morning visits (can be very hot midday)

### MacRitchie Reservoir
- **Type:** Reservoir park + nature trails
- **Highlights:** 10km trail around the reservoir, HSBC TreeTop Walk suspension bridge (booking required), turtles and long-tailed macaques
- **Cost:** Free (TreeTop Walk may require advance booking)

### East Coast Park
- **Type:** Coastal park
- **Highlights:** 15km park along the East Coast; cycling, barbecue pits, beach volleyball, seafood restaurants
- **Rentals:** Bicycles, rollerblades, kayaks
- **Cost:** Park is free; rentals from ~SGD 8/hour

### Southern Ridges
- **Type:** Park connector walk
- **Highlights:** 9km route connecting Mount Faber, Telok Blangah Hill Park, and Kent Ridge Park. Henderson Waves is Singapore's highest pedestrian bridge.
- **Cost:** Free

## Cultural & Heritage

### Chinatown
- **Highlights:** Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Sri Mariamman Temple (Hindu), Chinatown Heritage Centre, Chinatown Street Market, Pagoda Street, Smith Street
- **Best food:** Maxwell Food Centre, Chinatown Complex Food Centre
- **Best time:** Vibrant during Chinese New Year (January/February)

### Little India
- **Highlights:** Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, Sri Srinivasa Perumal Temple, Mustafa Centre, Tekka Centre, Serangoon Road shopping
- **Best food:** South and North Indian cuisines; roti prata, biryani, banana leaf rice
- **Best time:** Deepavali festival (October/November) for street lights and decorations

### Kampong Glam (Arab Quarter)
- **Highlights:** Sultan Mosque, Malay Heritage Centre, Haji Lane, Bussorah Street, Arab Street textiles
- **Best food:** Zam Zam Restaurant (murtabak), sup tulang (bone marrow soup) stalls
- **Best time:** Ramadan evenings for Geylang Serai bazaar

### National Museum of Singapore
- **Location:** Stamford Road, Civic District
- **Highlights:** Singapore's oldest museum; History Gallery, Living Galleries; interactive exhibits
- **Cost:** SGD 15 adults; free on Fridays 6–9pm
- **Best for:** History and culture enthusiasts

### Asian Civilisations Museum
- **Location:** Empress Place, Civic District (riverside)
- **Highlights:** Art and artefacts from Asia including Tang Shipwreck treasures
- **Cost:** SGD 20 adults

### Peranakan Museum
- **Location:** Armenian Street, Civic District
- **Highlights:** Dedicated to Peranakan (Straits Chinese) culture — intricate beadwork, clothing, ceramics
- **Cost:** SGD 10–15 adults

### Kampong Buangkok
- **Type:** Heritage site
- **Highlights:** Singapore's last rural village; old attap houses, community life — a rare glimpse of old Singapore

## Theme Parks & Attractions

### Universal Studios Singapore
- **Location:** Sentosa Island (Resorts World Sentosa)
- **Highlights:** 7 themed zones — Hollywood, New York, Sci-Fi City, Ancient Egypt, The Lost World, Far Far Away, Minion Land
- **Best rides:** Battlestar Galactica rollercoasters, Transformers 3D, Revenge of the Mummy
- **Cost:** ~SGD 83 adults, SGD 63 children; discounts available online
- **Best for:** Thrill-seekers, families, pop culture fans
- **Tip:** Arrive at opening time (10am) for shortest queues

### Night Safari
- **Location:** Mandai Wildlife Reserve
- **Highlights:** World's first nocturnal wildlife park; over 900 animals from 100 species; 40-minute tram ride + walking trails
- **Shows:** Creatures of the Night (7:30pm, 8:30pm, 9:30pm)
- **Cost:** ~SGD 55 adults, SGD 37 children
- **Best for:** Families, wildlife enthusiasts; unique experience

### Singapore Zoo
- **Location:** Mandai Wildlife Reserve
- **Highlights:** Open-concept design; free-ranging orangutans; Fragile Forest; Primate Kingdom; Wild Africa; Rainforest Fights Back show
- **Cost:** SGD 48 adults, SGD 33 children
- **Best for:** All ages; best visited in morning (7–11am) before midday heat

### River Wonders (River Safari)
- **Location:** Mandai Wildlife Reserve
- **Highlights:** World's first river-themed wildlife park; giant pandas Kai Kai and Jia Jia; Amazon River Quest boat ride
- **Cost:** SGD 42 adults, SGD 30 children

### S.E.A. Aquarium
- **Location:** Resorts World Sentosa, Sentosa Island
- **Highlights:** One of the world's largest aquariums; Open Ocean habitat with 40,000-litre tank; shark feeding dives available
- **Cost:** SGD 43 adults, SGD 33 children
- **Best for:** All ages, particularly good on rainy days

### Adventure Cove Waterpark
- **Location:** Resorts World Sentosa, Sentosa Island
- **Highlights:** Wave pool, lazy river, tube slides, Ray Bay snorkelling
- **Cost:** SGD 40 adults, SGD 33 children
- **Best for:** Active visitors, families; bring sunscreen

### Science Centre Singapore
- **Location:** Jurong East (near Chinese Garden MRT)
- **Highlights:** 1,000+ interactive exhibits across science, technology, engineering, mathematics; Omnimax Theatre; waterpark (H2O) adjacent
- **Cost:** SGD 15 adults, SGD 10 children
- **Best for:** Children and curious adults; rainy days

## Arts & Entertainment

### Esplanade – Theatres on the Bay
- Singapore's premier performing arts venue; hosts international and local productions
- Free outdoor performances frequently held on the waterfront; check website for schedule
- The rooftop terrace offers excellent views

### ArtScience Museum
- **Location:** Marina Bay Sands
- **Highlights:** Permanent "Future World" digital art exhibition; major international touring exhibitions
- **Cost:** varies by exhibition; from SGD 14
- **Best for:** Art and technology enthusiasts; families

### National Gallery Singapore
- **Location:** St Andrew's Road, Civic District (former Supreme Court and City Hall buildings)
- **Highlights:** Largest public collection of Southeast Asian art; colonial architecture
- **Cost:** SGD 20 adults; free on Fridays 6–9pm

### Gillman Barracks
- Contemporary art gallery cluster in converted colonial military barracks
- **Cost:** Free to browse; some galleries may charge for special exhibitions

## Beaches & Coastal

### Sentosa Island Beaches
- **Siloso Beach:** Most lively; beach bars, beach volleyball, activities
- **Palawan Beach:** Family-friendly; suspension bridge to Asia's southernmost point
- **Tanjong Beach:** Quieter; upscale beach club

### Palawan Beach Highlights
- Asia's most southernmost continental point on a small islet connected by suspension bridge
- Beach BBQ and picnic facilities

## Shopping

### Orchard Road
- Singapore's main shopping boulevard; over 20 malls in ~2.5km stretch
- Key malls: ION Orchard (luxury + mainstream), Ngee Ann City (Takashimaya), 313@Somerset (high street), Mandarin Gallery (boutiques), Paragon (luxury)

### VivoCity
- Singapore's largest mall; near HarbourFront MRT; gateway to Sentosa
- Rooftop Skypark with outdoor water area; IMAX cinema

### Bugis Street Market
- Popular budget shopping destination; fashion, accessories, souvenirs at negotiable prices

### Haji Lane
- Narrow street in Kampong Glam; boutique fashion, vintage stores, cafes, murals

### Tekka Centre (Little India)
- Wet market with fresh produce + hawker centre above; excellent Indian grocery shopping

## Food Experiences

### Signature Dish Experiences
- **Chilli Crab:** Best at Jumbo Seafood (Robertson Quay, Dempsey), No Signboard Seafood (VivoCity)
- **Hainanese Chicken Rice:** Tian Tian (Maxwell), Boon Tong Kee (multiple outlets)
- **Laksa:** 328 Katong Laksa (East Coast); Sungei Road Laksa (Little India)
- **Char Kway Teow:** Hill Street Char Kway Teow (Bedok)
- **Roti Prata:** Springleaf Prata Place (Upper Thomson)

### Hawker Centre Experiences
- **Lau Pa Sat Festival Market:** Victorian octagonal iron structure; Satay Street (7pm onwards)
- **Tiong Bahru Market:** Popular with locals; excellent breakfast options; neighbourhood cafes nearby
- **Bedok Interchange Hawker Centre:** Local favourite for late-night supper

### Food Tours
- Multiple organised food tours available through tour operators; covers multiple hawker centres and heritage neighbourhoods
- Walking food tours in Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong Glam

## Water Activities

### Kayaking / Stand-Up Paddleboard
- Available at East Coast Park, Sentosa, Pulau Ubin
- Rentals from ~SGD 20–35/hour

### White Water Rafting (Simulator)
- Freestyle Rafting at Adventure Cove Waterpark (Sentosa)

### Sailing / Dragon Boat
- Bedok Reservoir, Marina Bay (Dragon Boat Festival races in May/June)

## Day Trips and Nearby Islands

### Pulau Ubin
- Small island east of Singapore; accessible by bumboat from Changi Point Ferry Terminal (~SGD 3/person)
- Ideal for cycling through kampong (village) scenery, Chek Jawa wetlands boardwalk (mangroves, wildlife)
- No resorts; day trip only; bring water and snacks

### Sentosa Island
- Fully connected by multiple transport options; MRT (Harbourfront), Sentosa Express monorail, cable car, walking causeway
- Beach Shuttle bus within Sentosa is free
