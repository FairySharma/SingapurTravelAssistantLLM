# Visit Singapore: Sample Itineraries
Source: Visit Singapore — Sample Itineraries
URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/itinerary-planner/

## 1-Day Singapore Highlights Itinerary

### Morning (9am–12pm)
- **Marina Bay Waterfront Promenade:** Start your day with a stroll along the iconic waterfront. View the Merlion statue and take photos with the skyline as backdrop.
- **Gardens by the Bay:** Walk or taxi to this spectacular garden. Visit the free outdoor Supertree Grove (impressive even in daylight), then enter the Flower Dome — the world's largest glass greenhouse — for about 1.5 hours.

### Afternoon (12pm–6pm)
- **Lunch:** Take MRT or taxi to Chinatown; eat at Maxwell Food Centre. Try Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice (arrive before 12:30 to avoid long queues).
- **Chinatown Exploration:** Walk through the Chinatown Street Market, visit Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, and browse the shophouses. Visit Sri Mariamman Temple (Singapore's oldest Hindu temple).
- **Afternoon break:** Visit the National Museum of Singapore (air-conditioned, free on Friday evenings) or take a taxi to Little India for a browse through Tekka Centre.

### Evening (6pm–10pm)
- **Marina Bay Sands:** Head to the SkyPark Observation Deck for panoramic sunset views. A ticket is required for non-hotel guests.
- **Dinner:** Eat at Satay Street adjacent to Lau Pa Sat, or at one of the restaurants along Clarke Quay or Boat Quay.
- **Spectra Light Show:** Free light and water show at Marina Bay Sands Event Plaza — 8pm and 9pm shows (check schedule).
- **Supertree Light Show:** Catch the Garden Rhapsody light show at Gardens by the Bay (7:45pm and 8:45pm).

## 2-Day Culture & Heritage Itinerary

### Day 1: Colonial Singapore & Marina Bay

**Morning:**
- Begin at the Civic District — visit the National Museum of Singapore
- Walk through Fort Canning Park (historic hill, free entry)
- Esplanade — Theatres on the Bay (exterior walkway, free; check for free performances)
- Merlion Park photo stop

**Afternoon:**
- Lunch: Chinatown (Maxwell Food Centre)
- Clarke Quay: Walk along the restored riverside quay
- ArtScience Museum (advance booking recommended)

**Evening:**
- Marina Bay Sands SkyPark Observation Deck
- Gardens by the Bay — Supertree light show at 7:45pm
- Dinner at Gardens by the Bay restaurants or return to Clarke Quay

### Day 2: Ethnic Enclaves & Local Life

**Morning:**
- **Little India:** Start at Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple (one of Singapore's most colourful temples). Explore Serangoon Road. Breakfast at Komala Vilas or Tekka Centre (roti prata, thosai).
- Walk to **Kampong Glam:** Sultan Mosque (free entry; dress modestly), Malay Heritage Centre, Haji Lane (boutique shops and street art).

**Afternoon:**
- Lunch: Kampong Glam area or return to Arab Street hawker stalls
- **Chinatown:** Buddha Tooth Relic Temple museum (free), Chinatown Heritage Centre, shophouse browsing
- Tea break: Try traditional Singaporean kaya toast and kopi (coffee) at Ya Kun Kaya Toast

**Evening:**
- Clarke Quay or Boat Quay for dinner and waterside drinks
- Optional: Night walk along the Singapore River

## 3-Day Comprehensive Singapore Itinerary

### Day 1: Icons of Modern Singapore

**9am:** Arrive at Marina Bay area; Merlion Park and waterfront promenade
**10am:** Gardens by the Bay — Supertree Grove (free), then Cloud Forest dome (misty indoor rainforest, approximately 1.5 hours)
**12:30pm:** Lunch at Gardens by the Bay restaurants or take MRT to the nearby Chinatown area
**2pm:** ArtScience Museum at Marina Bay Sands
**4pm:** SkyPark Observation Deck (panoramic city views)
**6pm:** Check into hotel; freshen up
**7pm:** Dinner at Lau Pa Sat / Satay Street (try satay, hokkien mee, chicken rice)
**8pm:** Garden Rhapsody light show at Supertree Grove (free)

### Day 2: Cultural Singapore + Wildlife Night

**9am:** Little India — Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, morning breakfast at Tekka Centre
**10:30am:** Walk to Kampong Glam — Sultan Mosque, Haji Lane for shopping and photos
**12:30pm:** Lunch at Kampong Glam (Zam Zam Restaurant for murtabak; 115 years old)
**2pm:** Chinatown — Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Chinatown Heritage Centre, street market
**4pm:** Short MRT ride to Orchard Road — mall browsing and snacks
**6pm:** Return to hotel, rest and prepare for evening
**7pm:** Night Safari at Mandai Wildlife Reserve (tram + walking trails; allow 2–3 hours)
**After Night Safari:** Supper at nearby restaurant or return to Chinatown for supper

### Day 3: Sentosa Island Beach Resort Day

**9am:** Take MRT to HarbourFront, then Sentosa Express monorail to Sentosa Island
**9:30am–1pm:** Universal Studios Singapore (book tickets in advance; allow 3–4 hours minimum)
**1pm:** Lunch at Universal Studios or walk to Resorts World Sentosa Food Street
**2:30pm:** S.E.A. Aquarium or Adventure Cove Waterpark (choose based on preference)
**5pm:** Palawan Beach or Siloso Beach — sunset beach time
**7pm:** Return to main Singapore island
**8pm:** Farewell dinner at Orchard Road (Ion Sky restaurant) or Dempsey Hill (upscale options)

## Family with Children Itinerary (3 Days)

### Day 1: Wildlife Wonders

**Morning (9am–1pm):** Singapore Zoo
- Arrive at opening (9am); the zoo is best explored in the morning before afternoon heat
- Must-see: free-ranging orangutans, Fragile Forest (butterflies and small animals), Primate Kingdom
- Watch animal feeding times (check schedule at entrance)
- Breakfast or morning snack at the zoo's hawker area

**Afternoon (2pm–7pm):** Gardens by the Bay
- Cloud Forest dome — children love the misty waterfall and elevated walkways
- Flower Dome — beautiful and educational
- Outdoor Children's Garden (free, with water play area — bring a change of clothes)

**Evening:** Garden Rhapsody Supertree light show (7:45pm, free). Dinner at Gardens by the Bay.

### Day 2: Sentosa Fun Day

**Full Day at Sentosa Island:**
- Universal Studios Singapore — suitable for all ages; Minion Land and Sesame Street especially popular with young children
- S.E.A. Aquarium — mesmerising for all ages
- Adventure Cove Waterpark — great for older children (must be able to swim)
- Beach time at Palawan Beach (connected footbridge leads to Asia's southernmost point)
- iFly Singapore — indoor skydiving (minimum age and weight requirements apply)

### Day 3: Science & Nature

**Morning:** Science Centre Singapore (Jurong East MRT)
- Over 1,000 interactive exhibits; Omnimax Theatre (IMAX dome)
- Excellent for children interested in science and technology

**Afternoon:** Jurong Lake Gardens
- Singapore's first national gardens in the heartlands; playgrounds, lawns, bird-watching

**Evening:** Night Safari (Mandai)
- Children often love the nocturnal animals and the atmospheric jungle setting
- Creatures of the Night animal show at 7:30pm and 8:30pm

## Budget Traveller Itinerary (3 Days, Under SGD 150 for Activities)

### Day 1: Free & Cheap Singapore
- Merlion Park (free) + Singapore River walk (free)
- National Museum of Singapore (paid, but free on Friday evenings)
- Chinatown: Buddha Tooth Relic Temple (free) + street browsing
- Hawker meal: Maxwell Food Centre (~SGD 4–6)
- Gardens by the Bay outdoor areas (free) + Supertree light show (free)

### Day 2: Culture on a Budget
- Singapore Botanic Gardens (free UNESCO site; National Orchid Garden is ~SGD 5)
- Little India and Kampong Glam walking tour (free)
- Haw Par Villa (free) — quirky and unique
- East Coast Park cycling (bicycle rental ~SGD 8/hour)

### Day 3: Off the Beaten Track
- Pulau Ubin island: bumboat from Changi Point (~SGD 3 each way), cycling (~SGD 10/day)
- MacRitchie Reservoir boardwalk (free, 7km)
- Southern Ridges walk: Henderson Waves bridge to VivoCity (free, ~3 hours)

## Rainy Day Itinerary (Indoor Attractions)

Singapore's weather can be unpredictable. On wet or very hot days:

**Morning:**
- ArtScience Museum (Marina Bay Sands)
- The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands (luxury mall with canal inside)

**Afternoon:**
- National Museum of Singapore
- Chinatown Heritage Centre
- ION Orchard and Orchard Road malls (connected by underground walkways)

**Evening:**
- Gardens by the Bay Cloud Forest and Flower Dome
- Dinner at Lau Pa Sat (partially covered)

## Wellness & Nature Itinerary (2 Days)

### Day 1: Urban Green Spaces
- **Morning:** Singapore Botanic Gardens (UNESCO) — bring a picnic
- **Afternoon:** Southern Ridges walk (Telok Blangah Hill Park, Henderson Waves, Forest Walk)
- **Evening:** Dempsey Hill restaurants in former colonial barracks

### Day 2: Nature Reserves + Spa
- **Morning:** Bukit Timah Nature Reserve (Singapore's highest hill; free; guided hikes available)
- **Afternoon:** MacRitchie Reservoir TreeTop Walk (suspension bridge canopy walk; free but book in advance)
- **Evening:** Spa treatment in Chinatown or Clarke Quay; rooftop bar drinks with city view
